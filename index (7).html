📖 THE STORY OF THE CAUCA RIVER

The Cauca River is one of Colombia's great rivers. It travels roughly 1,300 km from the Andes to the Magdalena River, and for centuries it has given water, fish and trade to thousands of families. In the Bajo Cauca region of Antioquia — towns like Caucasia, Zaragoza and Nechí — the river is home, food and identity. Fishermen still pull silver bocachico from its waters.

So why is it sick? Illegal gold mining is the biggest wound. Miners use mercury to grab tiny flakes of gold, then wash the poison into the water. Mercury never disappears: small fish swallow it, then bigger fish, then people — and it can harm the brain, cause tremors and memory loss.

Mining and cutting down forests also push tons of mud and sediment into the river. The water turns brown-gold, sunlight can't reach the depths and fish run out of oxygen. Add garbage and untreated sewage from towns, and diseases like cholera spread.

But the story is not over. You are a SOLARYS Guardian with a Solar Launcher that fires pure light. Clean the trash, plant roots, break the mercury and defeat three giant pollution machines. The river is our territory — let's bring it back to life.
